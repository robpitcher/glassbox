## Who pays what

- **`mona` and `kai` hold GHEC licenses** because they're members of the `acme-web` organization. Under Copilot access, their Copilot seat costs $0.
- **`ray` has no GHEC license.** He gets Copilot through an enterprise team without joining an organization, so he uses Copilot Standalone at $10 per user per month.
- **Nobody gets included AI credits.** Every request is billed as metered usage against your budgets. Copilot Business ($19, 1,900 credits) and Copilot Enterprise ($39, 3,900 credits) add each seat's credits to a shared pool first. See <a href={withBase('/github/copilot-usage-billing/')}>Copilot usage-based billing</a> for how pools and budgets work.
- **Same features.** Copilot access gives the same features and models as Copilot Enterprise, subject to your policies.

## What stays the same

- **Owners still assign seats.** A GHEC license doesn't turn Copilot on. Owners assign it to members, enterprise teams, or organizations.
- **One model per enterprise.** Copilot access can't be combined with Business or Enterprise seats, even in a single organization. The two access options, through a GHEC license or through Standalone, can mix.
- **Budgets decide what keeps working.** If a budget runs out, code completions and next edit suggestions keep working, but features such as Chat, code review, and cloud agent need budget. Access is never removed.

## Switching to Copilot access

- **Voluntary.** It takes effect on the first of a month. For now, you switch through your account team; self-serve switching isn't available yet.
- **Not for every plan.** It's not available on Free or Team plans or on self-service GHEC trials.
- **Before you switch:** set budgets, keep the AI credits paid usage policy enabled, and allow `*.enterprise.githubcopilot.com` through your firewall.
- **Where people sit matters.** Whether someone has a GHEC license depends on organization membership. See <a href={withBase('/github/account-enterprise-hierarchy/')}>GitHub enterprise hierarchy</a>.
