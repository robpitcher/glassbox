## Who decides

- **The enterprise goes first.** For most Copilot policies, an enterprise owner chooses to enable the policy everywhere, disable it everywhere, or let organizations decide.
- **Organizations decide only when delegated.** If the enterprise enables or disables a policy, organization settings for it don't apply.
- **Your license source sets your policies.** A person follows the policies of the enterprise or organization that grants their Copilot Business or Enterprise license. `mona` follows `acme-web`.
- **Enterprise-assigned users have their own setting.** People licensed directly by the enterprise, like `ray` through an enterprise team, aren't covered by "Let organizations decide". The **Policies for enterprise-assigned users** setting chooses whether those policies default to enabled or disabled for them.
- **A few policies cover everyone.** For example, blocking Copilot in the cloud applies on the enterprise's repositories no matter where the user's license comes from.

## When licenses overlap

- **Across organizations, each policy has a rule.** When `kai` gets a license from both `acme-web` and `acme-data`, most policies (such as Copilot CLI, Chat in the IDE, agent mode, and MCP servers) use the least restrictive setting. A few (such as suggestions matching public code and the Copilot Metrics API) use the most restrictive.
- **Across enterprises, the most restrictive almost always wins.** AI credit paid usage and GitHub Spark are the exceptions.
- **Personal plans don't conflict.** Copilot Pro or Pro+ is cancelled when someone gets a Business or Enterprise seat.
- **Licensing itself** depends on where a person sits. See <a href={withBase('/github/account-enterprise-hierarchy/')}>GitHub enterprise hierarchy</a> and <a href={withBase('/github/copilot-access/')}>Copilot access with GHEC</a>.

## New features and models

- **Default policy for new features and clients** decides what happens to generally available features nobody has configured yet: let organizations decide, enable everywhere, or disable everywhere. It doesn't cover preview features.
- **Default availability for released models** does the same for models set to follow the default policy. Preview and some other models stay disabled until you enable them.

## Where to set them

- **Enterprise:** the **AI controls** tab, under Agents, Copilot, and MCP. Organizations use their own Copilot settings for anything delegated.
- **Watch for drift.** Check who holds policy access (enterprise owners and anyone with the "Manage enterprise AI controls" role), and review policy changes in the audit log.
- **Related:** <a href={withBase('/github/copilot-managed-settings/')}>Copilot managed settings</a> for client-side configuration, <a href={withBase('/github/copilot-request-lifecycle/')}>the lifecycle of a Copilot request</a> for where policies sit among the other controls, and <a href={withBase('/github/copilot-usage-billing/')}>Copilot usage-based billing</a> for budgets.
